- **Network Reciprocity (Section 13)**: Any institution or developer modifying this player and hosting it as a SaaS or web service must make the complete corresponding source code freely accessible to all remote users.

## 2. Using this Standalone Tool Legally

1. **Embedding via iFrame (No Modifications)**:
   Embedding `player/index.html` unmodified into your educational website, intranet, or classroom portal is fully permitted under AGPLv3.
2. **Modifying the Player Code**:
   If you alter `ast-engine.js`, `ast-compiler.js`, or `player-ui.js` and serve it to students over a network, you must release your modified player code under AGPLv3 or acquire a Track B license.
3. **Authoring Vector Scenes (`.svg` / `.ast`)**:
   Educational slide decks and scenes authored using the player remain the intellectual property of their creators.
